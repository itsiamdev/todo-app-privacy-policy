01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Todo handles information when you use our Todo List application. Todo is a simple productivity app and is built with privacy in mind.

By using Todo, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

Todo does not collect any information from you. The application does not collect personal data, usage data, analytics data, or information about your device.

03

Personal Data

Todo does not collect or request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, financial information, passwords, or any other personal information. Todo does not use accounts or authentication.

04

Local Data Storage

If you create tasks or other content in Todo, that information is stored only locally on your device, if applicable. It is not transmitted to us, uploaded to a server, or made available to third parties.

We do not operate servers for storing user data, and we do not have access to content saved locally by the application.

05

Data Sharing

Because Todo does not collect data, it does not share, sell, rent, or disclose personal information or device data to the developer or to any third party.

06

Third-Party Services

Todo does not use third-party services for collecting, processing, analyzing, or storing user data. The application does not use Firebase, Supabase, or any other service for user data collection.

07

Advertising and Analytics

Todo does not display advertisements and does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or similar tools. No usage information is collected to measure activity or build user profiles.

08

Data Security

Since Todo does not collect or transmit data, there is no user data held by us to secure or manage. Any tasks saved by the application remain subject to the security and settings of your own device.

09

Children's Privacy

Todo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The application does not knowingly collect information from users of any age.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
